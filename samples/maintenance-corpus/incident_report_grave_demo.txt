Incident severity: Serious injury / potential fatality

Description
During a die change, the press ram moved unexpectedly while a maintenance worker's left hand was inside the guarded tooling area. The worker sustained a severe crush injury to the hand and was transported to the site medical unit. No fatality occurred in this demonstration record. The exact movement cause has not yet been confirmed.

Immediate actions taken
- The emergency stop was activated and the machine was isolated.
- Lockout-Tagout was applied after the movement stopped.
- The area was barricaded and the Shift Supervisor was notified.
- Plant Safety Officer and the site medical team were notified.
- Work Order WO-DEMO-8821 was opened with Critical status.
- The scene was preserved for investigation. Do not restart the press until the investigation lead releases it.

Initial observations
- The maintenance log records an accumulator pressure of 171 bar during the latest routine check.
- The related preventive-maintenance SOP states that the accumulator should be depressurized below 5 bar before seal inspection.
- A hydraulic fluid leak was previously noted at Seal #4.
- The die-change permit and isolation checklist were not present at the work area when the scene was secured.
- CCTV review and witness interviews are pending.

Potential contributing conditions — preliminary only
- Stored hydraulic energy may not have been fully released before the die-change activity.
- The isolation verification step may not have been completed or documented.
- The guard/interlock state and control-system event log require technical verification.
- The previous seal condition and accumulator-pressure trend require review against SOP-PM-014 and MLF-03.

Required investigation questions
1. Was the press fully isolated and verified before the worker entered the tooling area?
2. What pressure and stored-energy conditions existed at the time of the movement?
3. Did any guard, interlock, valve, sensor, or control fault contribute?
4. Which permit, LOTO, and die-change steps were completed and who verified them?
5. Do the maintenance log and SOP require escalation or equipment quarantine beyond the actions already taken?

Recommended containment pending investigation
- Keep HP-800-03 out of service.
- Maintain the scene and preserve CCTV, controller logs, permits, and witness statements.
- Confirm medical follow-up and notify the required internal safety contacts.
- Require a documented safety review before any restart or test movement.
