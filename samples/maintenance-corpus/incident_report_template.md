# Incident Report — INC-2026-0912
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential
**Reported By:** T-1187
**Date/Time:** 2026-09-08 14:22
**Asset:** HP-800-03, Line 3

## Description
During shift-B routine check, vibration reading at bearing housing B2 recorded 5.6 mm/s RMS,
exceeding the 4.5 mm/s RMS threshold defined in SOP-PM-014. Accumulator pressure measured at
171 bar, approaching the 170 bar minimum. Seal #4 shows an active hydraulic fluid leak.

## Immediate Action Taken
- Machine placed under LOTO.
- Work Order WO-4471 status updated to "Critical."
- Shift Supervisor and Plant Safety Officer notified per SOP-PM-014 Escalation Matrix.

## Root Cause (Preliminary)
Suspected progressive seal degradation (Seal Kit SK-800-C) not replaced during last scheduled
changeout (ref. Maintenance Log MLF-03, entries 2026-08-18 through 2026-09-08).

## Recommended Corrective Action
1. Replace Seal #4 immediately (Seal Kit SK-800-C).
2. Re-pressurize accumulator to nominal 180-195 bar range.
3. Review PM interval for seal inspection — consider reducing from weekly to bi-weekly given
   recurrence pattern.
