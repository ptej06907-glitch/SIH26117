# Standard Operating Procedure — SOP-PM-014
## Hydraulic Press Line 3: Weekly Preventive Maintenance

**Department:** Press Shop, Plant 2
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential
**Revision:** 4.2
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-01

### 1. Purpose
This SOP defines the weekly preventive maintenance (PM) checks for the 800-tonne hydraulic
stamping press (Asset ID: HP-800-03) to prevent unplanned downtime and hydraulic seal failure.

### 2. Scope
Applies to all certified maintenance technicians on Line 3, shifts A and B.

### 3. Safety Requirements
- Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) must be applied before any panel is opened.
- Hydraulic accumulator must be depressurized to below 5 bar before seal inspection.
- PPE: face shield, insulated gloves, steel-toe boots.

### 4. Procedure
1. Verify hydraulic fluid level is between MIN and MAX marks on sight glass.
2. Inspect all seals (Ref: Seal Kit SK-800-C) for weeping or discoloration.
3. Check accumulator pre-charge pressure — nominal range 180-195 bar.
4. Log vibration reading at bearing housing B2 using handheld accelerometer.
   - Alert threshold: > 4.5 mm/s RMS
5. Inspect ram guide rails for scoring; apply lubricant per Lube Chart LC-09.
6. Record all readings in Maintenance Log Form MLF-03.
7. If any reading exceeds threshold, raise a Work Order and escalate to Shift Supervisor.

### 5. Escalation Matrix
| Condition | Action | Escalate To |
|---|---|---|
| Vibration > 4.5 mm/s RMS | Stop machine, raise WO | Shift Supervisor |
| Accumulator pressure < 170 bar | Schedule repressurization | Maintenance Lead |
| Seal weeping observed | Replace within 48 hrs | Maintenance Lead |
| Any LOTO violation observed | Immediate stop-work | Plant Safety Officer |
